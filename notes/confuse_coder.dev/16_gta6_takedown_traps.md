# GTA 6 clips: 3 takedown traps (DEV 16)

Checked 19 Nov 2026 on Rockstar's own page: [Policy on posting copyrighted Rockstar Games material](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/7bNaeoMFTV0iUDGhStTXvz/policy-on-posting-copyrighted-rockstar-games-material) (last updated 10 Jan 2025; it doesn't name GTA 6, it covers all Rockstar games).

## What is OK
- Rockstar says it is "happy for fans to upload footage of their gameplay".
- Cutscenes **inside** a longer play-through or a narrated Let's Play are OK.

## Trap 1: isolated cutscenes
- A clip that shows the ending, or that joins cutscenes / plot dialogue together, can be taken down.
- Policy: "isolated videos of the game's cinematics will be removed".

## Trap 2: in-game TV shows and comedy, posted alone
- Policy: "any posting of these in isolation will be flagged for removal".

## The money line
- "Non-commercial" = you don't make money through the game footage you post.
- Clips made for paid clipping campaigns are outside that. Rockstar "reserves the right to have its copyrighted material taken down at any time".

## Trap 3: the car radio (not Rockstar at all)
- In-game radio songs belong to music labels, not Rockstar.
- If a song plays in your clip, YouTube's Content ID can send a copyright claim, whatever Rockstar's policy says.
- No confirmed GTA 6 "streamer mode" that mutes licensed songs yet.

## Before you post (checklist)
- [ ] No ending / spoiler-only cutscene clip
- [ ] No in-game TV / comedy show posted on its own
- [ ] Radio OFF while recording (or cut the parts with songs)
- [ ] Upload as **Private** first
- [ ] Wait for YouTube Studio **Checks** to finish (it screens "for ad suitability and copyright claims before publishing", [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7561938))
- [ ] Never post leaked / pre-release footage (always taken down)

Unsure if a scene counts as a spoiler? Rockstar's page says to email copyright@take2games.com with the scene and its context.
