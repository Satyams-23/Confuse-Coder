# ScaleReach fact check: 4 claims vs its own website
*@confuse__coder.dev · DEV 17*

---

A viral reel promoted [ScaleReach](https://www.scalereach.ai/) on the GTA 6 launch. I checked its 4 big claims on 19 Nov 2026, using the tool's **own** website and the pages the reel showed.

| Claim in the viral reel | What the source says | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "6, 8, 10 hour streams, easily" | ScaleReach FAQ: "Up to 30 minutes per file on the Free plan and up to 3 hours on Pro and Agency." (Its steps section says 4 hours, so the site disagrees with itself.) | Half true |
| "Last week's #1 startup in the world" | [unicor.ne](https://www.unicor.ne/) ranks only startups listed on TrustMRR, by revenue **growth %** (7d/30d/90d). On 18 Nov ScaleReach was **#8**. | Half true |
| "The same videos that get MrBeast millions of views" | The site says "Perfect for MrBeast…". That's a list of big channels, not a customer list. | False |
| "Clipping campaigns pay $10-20 per 1,000 views" | The GTA 6 campaign on Content Rewards: **$0.70 per 1K views**, max **$35** per clip (19 Nov). More in DEV 11 notes. | False |

## Plan math (from ScaleReach's FAQ)
| Plan | Price | Minutes / month | Max per file |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 100 (watermark) | 30 min |
| Pro | $18/mo, or $12.50/mo yearly | 400 | 3 hours |
| Agency | $99/mo, or $49/mo yearly | 5,000 | 3 hours |

- Billing counts **minutes of source video**, not clips.
- One 8-hour stream = 480 minutes → more than Pro's whole month (400). You also have to split it into 3 files (3h + 3h + 2h).
- Agency's 5,000 minutes ≈ 10 such streams a month.

## What's genuinely good
- About $0.03 per source minute on Pro yearly (their own number).
- Scheduler included (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, LinkedIn), gaming split-screen layout, MCP server for Claude/Cursor, 100+ caption languages.

## How to test before paying
1. Use the 100 free minutes (no card needed).
2. Upload one **30-minute** part of your own stream (Free's per-file limit).
3. Check the clips: hook, crop on your face, captions in Hinglish. Watermark is expected on free.
4. Only pay if the clips are good. Check the minutes you'll need per month first: stream hours × 60.
