# Gaming clips ka paisa: kaun deta hai
*@confuse__coder.dev · DEV 21 · Reel: 10 lakh views, kamaai ₹0?*

---

## 1. Idea in one line
Gaming clip pe views aana aur paisa milna alag cheezein hain. Apps khud clippers ko kam ya zero dete hain; asli paisa clipping campaigns se aata hai.

> Live check: 20 Nov 2026. Rules aur campaigns badalte rehte hain, post karne se pehle official page khud kholo.

## 2. App by app: kaun kya deta hai
**Instagram (India)**
- Reels views pe abhi koi open per-view bonus nahi hai.
- Gifts aur Subscriptions se kamaai ho sakti hai, par unki eligibility alag hai (Professional Dashboard → Monetisation mein dekho).
- "Reels bonus open hai, DM karo" wale messages **scam** hain.

**Twitch**
- Doosre streamer ke clips banane wale ko Twitch **kuch nahi deta**.
- Paisa sirf streamer kamaata hai, Affiliate bankar.

**YouTube Shorts**
- Shorts se ad revenue milta hai, par pehle YouTube Partner Program (YPP) mein aana hoga.
- YPP ke baad bhi gaming Shorts pe 10 lakh views = bas kuch hazaar rupaye.
- **Sirf doosron ke clips re-upload** karne se monetization nahi milta. Apni awaaz, commentary ya edit jodo.

**Kick**
- Streamer N3on ne ek 5 hafte ke period mein 303 clippers ko $1.4 million se zyada diye (~₹12 crore). (source: Business Insider, via Tubefilter, Apr 2026)
- Ye Kick ka public per-view rate nahi tha. **Ye streamer ka apna clipping budget tha.**

**TikTok:** India mein banned hai, isliye ye option nahi.

## 3. Criteria (official pages, 20 Nov 2026)
**Twitch Affiliate** (source: help.twitch.tv, "Joining the Affiliate Program")
| Shart | Kitna |
|---|---|
| Followers | 25 |
| Streaming | 4 ghante |
| Alag din | 4 |
| Average viewers | 3 (last 30 din mein, 4 stream days pe) |

**YouTube Partner Program** (source: support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- 1,000 subscribers **aur** 90 din mein 1 crore (10 million) qualified Shorts views.
- Ya 1,000 subscribers + 12 mahine mein 4,000 qualified watch hours (long videos).
- 1 Feb 2027 se YPP mein updates aa rahe hain. Naye numbers YouTube ke update page pe check karo.

## 4. Asli opportunity: clipping campaigns
Campaign mein ek streamer ya brand apna budget rakhta hai, aur clippers ko verified views ke hisaab se pay karta hai. Example marketplace: Content Rewards.

```text
kamaai = views / 1000 × rate
         (max payout per clip tak)
minimum payout se kam = $0
platform fee ≈ 10%
```

Example: $1 per 1,000 views, minimum payout $2. Clip pe 1,500 views aaye → **$0**, kyunki 2,000 views ke bina minimum nahi pahunchta.

## 5. Teen steps
1. **Campaign chuno.** Check karo: rate per 1,000 views, minimum views, maximum payout, aur India ke views eligible hain ya nahi.
2. **Usi campaign ke streamer ka clip banao**, aur campaign ke brief mein bataaye platform par post karo.
3. **Post ka link submit karo.** Approval aur verified views ke baad payout milta hai, 10% platform fee katke.

## 6. Common mistakes
- Kisi bhi famous streamer ka clip daal ke paisa expect karna. Paisa sirf usi streamer ke clip ka milta hai jo campaign chala raha hai.
- Brief na padhna: platform, country aur clip rules har campaign ke alag hote hain.
- Fake views ya bots: clip reject, payout zero.
- "Bonus open" DMs pe bharosa karna.
