# Creative direction — <song title>

## Palette (whole video, nothing else)
- Paper: cream `#F3EAD7` (+ blotches, fibre grain)
- Ink: charcoal `#2A2724` (lines, most words)
- Accent: red `#C8352B`, ONLY for the key word of each line and that line's key object

## Style
Hand-drawn ink sketch on warm paper. Lines draw themselves, "boil" slightly every 3 frames, double sketchy
outline. Slow camera drift on every scene, paper layer parallax 0.3, subject 1.0, text 0.6–0.8.
Text: Mukta 800, whole words, appears on its timestamp. Film grain + vignette on top.
